# NoteForces Privacy Policy

NoteForces does not collect, transmit or sell any data.

- Your notes, highlights and drawings are stored only in your browser's local extension storage on your own device.
- If you choose a notes folder or export a backup, those files are written only to the location you pick on your own computer.
- NoteForces has no servers, accounts, analytics, tracking or advertising, and it makes no network requests.
- The extension only runs on Codeforces problem pages (`codeforces.com`) so it can show its tools there.

Uninstalling NoteForces deletes the data it stored in your browser. Files you saved to a folder or exported are not affected.
